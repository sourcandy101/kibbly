# Dog photo and video collection guide

For friends and family who lend their dog to test Kibbly recognition. Takes about 10 minutes per dog.

## Before you start

1. Ask the owner for permission. Explain that the photos are used only to test the Kibbly prototype, stay private (never on GitHub or social media) and are deleted on request.
2. Use a phone camera with normal settings: no filter, no portrait mode, 720p or 1080p video.

## What to record for each dog

| # | Item | How |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Eating video, 30 to 60 seconds** | The most important item. Phone on the floor or on a box about 30 to 40 cm high, 50 cm in front of the bowl, pointing at where the dog's head will be. Start recording, then put food in the bowl. |
| 2 | Walking up video, 15 seconds | Same phone position, the dog walks toward the bowl from 2 or 3 meters away. |
| 3 | 5 photos | Front of the face, left side, right side, from above while eating, whole body. |
| 4 | Second light | Repeat item 1 once in daylight and once in the evening with indoor light, if possible. |
| 5 | Collar (later) | When the colored collars arrive, repeat item 1 with the collar on. |

Also useful, once per session: 10 seconds of the empty bowl, and 10 seconds of a person standing in front of the bowl. These test that Kibbly serves nothing to people or to an empty scene.

## Information to note

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| Dog name | Rocky |
| Owner (first name) | Sami |
| Breed | Mixed |
| Coat colors | black and tan |
| Approximate age | 3 years |
| Collar color (if any) | none |
| Date and light | 9 November, evening indoor |

## How to send the files

Put everything for one dog in a folder named `<dog name>_<owner first name>` (for example `rocky_sami`) with a text file of the information above, and send it to Zaineb by Google Drive or USB. Zaineb stores it in `D:\kibbly_data\friends\`.

## Goal

At least 3 dogs, ideally 2 of them of the same breed or color (the hard case).
